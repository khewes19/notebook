How it works

Pitches come from OpenStreetMap (leisure=pitch). Check in within 250 m to claim one. Challenges record a camera clip on your phone and log the number you enter. Automatic counting is a later layer.

Missing a pitch? Add it on openstreetmap.org and it shows up for everyone.
